How scores work

Every check has a weight: critical 3, major 2, minor 1. The score is the share of weight that passes. Products and collections follow the product and collection checklists. Blog articles are checked for:

  • Critical: 600+ words, keyword in title, first sentence and an H2/H3, density 1-1.5%, meta description 150-160 characters, English handle (2-5 words, 43 characters max), featured image with alt text, internal links to active products, no medical-sounding wording, no negative or uncertain sentences, no prices.
  • Major: 2+ product links, a neutral external link, 3+ headings, meta title 60 characters max with the keyword early, keyword in meta description, no stop words in the handle, at least one tag.
  • Minor: sentences under 20 words, meta title different from meta description, alt text 125 characters max.
